title: ETS2/ATS Mod Status (1.61)

The 1.61 update is now available for American Truck Simulator and Euro Truck Simulator 2.

It appears that all currently supported mods for Euro Truck Simulator 2 and American Truck Simulator work without issues with the new update.

I plan on releasing a collection of small mods for both games that take advantage of some of the new features that 1.61 brings, mainly in preparation for the Road Trip expansion for ATS.

You can see all mods [here](/docs/ets2_ats/).

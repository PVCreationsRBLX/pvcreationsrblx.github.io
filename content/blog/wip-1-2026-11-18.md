Today, I wanted to share with you all a few projects I'm working on.

## Bus Driver-inspired paint jobs
I've wanted to create paint jobs inspired by [Bus Driver](https://store.steampowered.com/app/302080/Bus_Driver/), a cult classic created by a much smaller SCS that brought myself and others endless joy and frustration as a child, for the ETS2 Coaches DLC ever since it was first announced. It's also their first independently published game! Though I've made skins before, mainly for Rigs of Rods and OMSI, the new Coaches DLC proved to be quite a challenge, especially with how the UVs are laid out. At least for the Lion's Coach.

I plan on creating numerous paint jobs for all existing coaches in the DLC. Some coaches may have unique paint jobs. I have yet to finalize exact details.

![s](/images/devlogs/2026-11-18/eut2_hq_6afbb4e6.webp)
I call this paint job ***Laetiporus***, after *Laetiporus sulphureus*, an edible bracket fungus that reportedly tastes like chicken (hence it being commonly called chicken of the woods). I'll probably change the name of this paint job, though I'm not opposed to chucking in some Latin whenever I can. Due to this paint job's relative simplicity (just bodyside stripes), I was able to incorporate much of this paint job's definitions into a single def (.sii) file. I created a separate texture for the front bumper mainly to ~~hide my sloppy texture work~~ prevent bleeding and blank spots, primarily, again, due to how the Lion's Coach's UVs were made.

## Scania Touring tweaks
I noticed SCS changed quite a few things about the Touring during development, namely the wiper cowl material. It went from painted (mid-2025) to plastic, then to glossy at release. Research showed the Touring could be specced with either. I didn't like the glossy cowl, so I brought back the plastic cowl as an option since the cowl is already its own accessory! I might adjust the actual wiper materials as well.

Fun fact: the Touring is the only coach in the DLC that required me to stop for fuel mid-route.

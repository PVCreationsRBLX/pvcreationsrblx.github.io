title: ETS2/ATS Mod Status (1.60)
---

Just over a month after the release of 1.59, 1.60 is available for American Truck Simulator and Euro Truck Simulator 2.

* None of the vanilla S-Way Quick Jobs configs use metallic colors anymore, but they still don't have retarders, so the overrides in [ETS2-QJTI](/docs/ets2_ats/ets2/ets2-qjti/) will stick.
* [Extra VNL Parts](/docs/ets2_ats/ats/extra_vnl_parts/) was updated to fix an issue where the newly added mirror text was just nonsense.

You can see all mods [here](/docs/ets2_ats/).
